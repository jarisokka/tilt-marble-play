How to play

  • Roll the marble from the start pad to the checkered goal.
  • Phone: tap Enable Tilt & Play, allow motion access, hold still while it calibrates, then lean the phone. Lean right to roll right on screen, tip the top edge away to roll up the screen. The grip you hold at GO is level, so you can settle your grip during every countdown. Tap ⟲ any time to re-level by hand.
  • Touch: put a finger anywhere on the course and drag. Lift to let go.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD. P / Esc pauses, R restarts, C re-calibrates, M mutes.
  • Falling off costs a life and puts you back at the last flag. Three lives per course.
  • Blue gems take 2 seconds off your time. Beat the par time and keep every life for three stars.
  • Orange blocks slide, yellow gates rise (they flash first), red arms sweep. Pink bumpers kick.
  • Downhill Rush: no lives, a race against the clock instead. Falling off costs time, gems add 3 seconds, and the time you have left carries over to the next course. Small step-downs are fine, but a drop of more than about 4 marble-heights shatters the marble. Dark marbles with eyes are rivals: they roll straight at you.
